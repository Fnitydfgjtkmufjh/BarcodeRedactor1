Pages without any detected barcode are always flagged for review – they are never assumed safe.

**Limits of this evaluation:** pages are synthetic (rendered Code 128 symbols plus simulated degradations). Real print-and-scan artefacts (toner spread, paper texture, scanner-specific thresholding) differ, so validate on a sample of your own scanned documents before relying on the tool, and keep the review workflow on.
